# RP rules

For all roleplay/story work in this repo (`rp/**`).

## Writing

**Tone is always flirty, sexy, naughty and bold** — every chapter, every story, every locale. She
teases, provokes, owns the room, and says the thing a normal person wouldn't. Never sanitize a beat
into something polite; if a line can be hotter, make it hotter.

**Very simple day-to-day language. No complex grammar.** Short sentences, common words, contractions,
natural spoken rhythm, fragments allowed, one beat per line. No literary or bookish words, no
three-clause sentences, no archaic English in period settings (*thee, thou, prithee, whilst* — never).
A 16–30 year old native speaker must understand every line instantly on first read. Period and
fantasy formality comes from how characters address each other (Highness, servant, Mistress, kneel),
never from old grammar.

Stories are **linear** — choices change her immediate reply, then reconverge. No vars, no locked
content.

## Visuals

**The female lead is always sexy, revealing, hot and bold — in every image prompt, every video
prompt, every chapter, every story, no exceptions.** Plunging neckline, deep cleavage, bare shoulders
and back, high slit with bare leg, thin clinging fabric, cinched waist, confident dominant posture,
direct eye contact. If a scene would realistically call for modest clothing, cut it lower and open it
further instead.

All media is **9:16 vertical** (covers, background loops, chapter stills, chapter videos). One still
per chapter → one `.mkv` generated from that still, same base name. The male player character's face
is never visible.

## The line

Bold and revealing but **clothed and non-explicit** — fade at the line, nothing that would block a
store listing. All characters are adults.

## Full detail

- `rp/WRITING_RULES.md` — tone, language level, choice and structure conventions
- `rp/MEDIA_RULES.md` — per-genre reveal table, format, lighting, prompt conventions

Each story's own `STYLE.md` / `MEDIA.md` inherits these and may only strengthen them, never soften
them.
