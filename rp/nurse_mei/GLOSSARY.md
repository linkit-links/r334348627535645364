## Names & brand

- Character first name stays **Mei** in Latin script for id/pt; **Мэй** for ru; **मेई** for hi (Mei acceptable in id/pt dialogue if natural).
- Do **not** translate: node ids, `set` keys (`friendly`, `humor`, `slowburn`, `curiosity`, …), URLs, emoji.

## Gender

- Player: male-coded patient (he/him in EN). Agree adjectives in pt/ru/hi accordingly.
- Mei: she/her → dia (id), ela (pt), она (ru), वो/वह (hi).
