# Style — Doctor's Office (Lucy)

Simple everyday language. Flirty / sexy / hot — match English heat exactly. Do **not** sanitize, do **not** go more explicit than EN.

## Reading level

- Short sentences, common words, natural speech (chat / soap / dating-app energy)
- Avoid textbook grammar and rare literary words
- Would a 16–30yo native understand every line quickly?

## Voice split

| Voice | How it sounds |
|-------|----------------|
| **narrator** | Second-person “you”, cinematic but simple |
| **Lucy** (nodes with no `speaker`) | Warm, teasing, slightly bossy doctor energy — thorough, in control, playful |

## Heat curve

Soft tease early (CH01–03) → rising tension (CH04–07) → hot / dominant later (CH08–10 Mistress, no mercy) → tender “I love you” / proposal (CH11).

## Keep always

- Name **Lucy** / **Dr. Lucy**
- Medical-care frame (doctor, exam, checkup, patient, clinic)
- Markdown `*emphasis*` markers (translate words inside)
- Emojis placement similar to EN (🙂 😏 🩺 💓 etc.)
- Line breaks = separate `lines[]` entries (one beat per line)
- Player male-coded patient; Lucy she/her — gender agreement in PT/RU/HI
