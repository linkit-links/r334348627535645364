THE DOCTOR'S OFFICE — MEDIA PROMPTS

Character: Dr. Lucy — young, beautiful doctor. Warm, confident, seductive. Dark eyes, soft warm smile, hair down. Wears a white coat over a fitted top. Setting: an upscale, softly-lit private exam room (warm walls, dim amber lighting, exam table with paper covering).

Patient (the viewer, "you") — appears from patient POV where possible; when shown, a young man in a paper exam gown on the table.

For each scene: Scene = what happens in plain English. Image prompt and Video prompt = generation prompts.
