# Visual rules — ALL roleplays (global)

Applies to every story folder under `rp/`: existing (`doctors_office`, `nurse_mei`,
`captured_princess_elara`, `yui`, `sasha_secret_agent`, `gym_trainer`, `same_bed_weekend`) and every
new one. Each story's own `MEDIA.md` inherits these rules and may only make them *stronger*, never
softer.

## Rule 1 — The female lead is always sexy, revealing, hot and bold

Non-negotiable, in every image and every video, in every chapter, regardless of what the scene is
about. Court, hospital, gym, prison cell, funeral, gunfight, grief — she is dressed to be looked at.

If a beat would realistically put her in something modest, the answer is **no**:
- The neckline goes lower. Deep plunge, deep cleavage, bare shoulders and collarbones.
- The hem goes higher, or the gown gets a slit to the hip with a bare leg out.
- The robe/coat/shirt is open, not closed. If she's holding it closed, it's failing.
- Fabric is thin, clinging, wet, or silk. It shows every curve.
- Waist is cinched — corset, sash, belt, tight bodice.
- Legs, back, and shoulders are bare wherever the garment allows.

Never a covered chest. Never a closed neckline. Never a shapeless silhouette.

## Rule 2 — Bold, not passive

She holds the frame. Direct eye contact with camera or open ownership of whoever she's looking at.
Chin level or lifted. Confident posture — lounging, leaning, standing over someone, one hand on a
hip or in someone's hair. No shy poses, no hunched shoulders, no hands covering herself, no coy
looking-away unless the story beat explicitly calls for it.

## Rule 3 — Every genre has a revealing answer

| Scene type | Minimum reveal |
|------------|----------------|
| Formal / court / office / public | Backless or open-laced back, plunge to the waist, bare shoulders, slit to the hip |
| Uniform (nurse, doctor, agent, trainer) | Uniform worn unbuttoned low and fitted tight, sleeves pushed up, hem short, stockings |
| Outdoor / travel / riding | Coat worn open over a low shift or bra top, bare legs, boots and thigh |
| Night / bed | Thin-strap silk slip off both shoulders, sheets low, bare legs and back |
| Bath / water | Wet skin, wet clinging fabric, steam, silhouette, bare shoulders and collarbones |
| Soft / hurt / grieving | His open shirt over bare skin, or a robe barely closed |
| Punishment / dominance | Corset and stockings, silk disordered, sleeves pushed up |
| Action / danger | Fitted and torn, bare midriff or thigh holster, sweat, clothing pulled open |

## Rule 4 — Format

- **Everything 9:16 vertical** — covers, background loops, reference sheets, chapter stills, chapter
  videos. Put `9:16 vertical` in the prompt and `landscape orientation` in the negative.
- One still per chapter → one `.mkv` generated from that still, same base name.
- Male lead / player character: **face never visible**. Over-the-shoulder, back of head, or hands.
- Single-source warm light from one side, deep shadow on the other. Skin has shine. Never flat front
  lighting.
- Video motion stays small: breath, fabric settling, flame, one deliberate hand movement.

## Rule 5 — The line

Bold and revealing, **clothed and non-explicit**. No nudity, nothing that would block a store
listing. Suggestive is the target; explicit is not. If a model refuses a prompt, soften the *wording*
("revealing silk gown, plunging neckline") — never cover her up.

All characters are adults. Use adult-appearance descriptors in every prompt.
